Suno is bringing one of its most personal AI music features to phones. **Voices is now available in the Suno mobile app on both iOS and Android**, allowing creators to record a voice, select an existing Voice profile, and use it directly in AI-generated songs without moving to the desktop experience.

The mobile rollout turns a feature introduced with [Suno v5.5](https://suno.com/release-notes/introducing-v5-5-voices-custom-models-and-my-taste) into a much more portable workflow. Voices can capture the characteristics of a creator's own vocal recording and apply them to new Suno songs. Suno's help documentation says users can create a Voice from a real-time recording or an existing audio file, with clean acapella recordings generally producing the best input.

## From voice memo to AI song

On mobile, the process is deliberately simple. Open **Create**, tap **+Voices**, then choose an existing Voice or create a new one. That means a rough vocal idea recorded on a phone can become the starting point for a more complete AI-generated track without first exporting files to a computer.

This matters because AI music creation has increasingly moved beyond text prompts. A prompt can describe genre, mood, or instrumentation, but a recognizable human voice gives the result a stronger personal identity. By putting Voices directly into the mobile creation screen, Suno is reducing the gap between capturing an idea and turning it into a finished song.

According to the [mobile launch announcement](https://www.reddit.com/r/SunoAI/comments/1vi22b2/voices_just_landed_on_mobile/), free users receive a limited version of Voices, while paid plans receive full access. Suno's own v5.5 materials identify **Pro and Premier** as the subscription tiers for the full Voices experience.

## A more personal Suno workflow

Voices is not simply a generic vocal preset. Suno requires voice verification when a new Voice is created, and its [Voices documentation](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/11362369) recommends using a clear recording for better results. Availability can also vary by location.

The larger shift is straightforward: Suno is making personalized AI music creation less dependent on a desktop setup. For creators who sketch lyrics, melodies, or vocal ideas on their phones, the ability to move directly from a recording to an AI song makes the mobile app significantly more useful as a complete creation tool.
