# Community collection policy

Treat registered community sources as ordinary daily sources rather than a second research phase. Community material is an evidence layer inside the article, not a separate page section.

- Collect only community entries inside the same New York 24-hour window.
- Publish an independent AI-related community event as a signal with `origin: "community"`.
- Group community posts about the same underlying event by content and publish one signal per event.
- Attach useful discussion, tests, maintainer clarification, compatibility reports, or adoption evidence about an existing news event to that signal's `communityVoices`.
- Do not attach posts that merely restate a headline or add no concrete observation.
- Preserve the collected community URL exactly as supplied.
- Do not perform targeted follow-up searches, platform availability checks, popularity scoring, evidence scoring.
- Zero community voices is valid when configured sources contain no useful in-window observations. Never manufacture community consensus.
