# Community collection policy

Treat registered community sources as ordinary daily sources rather than a second research phase.

- Collect only community entries inside the same New York 24-hour window.
- Publish a community entry when it is an independent AI-related event, discussion, test, maintainer clarification, compatibility report, or adoption signal.
- Group community posts about the same underlying event by content and publish one story per event.
- Keep discussion that merely repeats a selected news announcement inside that news event group instead of publishing it again.
- Use `kind: "community"` for selected independent community events.
- Preserve the collected community URL exactly as supplied.
- Do not perform targeted follow-up searches, platform availability checks, popularity scoring, evidence scoring.
- Zero community stories is valid when the configured community sources contain no independent in-window events.
